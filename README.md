# Remaining Human

A standardized, unnormed research battery measuring four cognitive capacities: vigilant attention,
executive attention, working memory, and sustained attention.

Everything runs in the browser. There is no account, no server, and no upload: every response stays
in local storage on the device that produced it.

**What this is not.** It is not normed, so no percentile or ranking exists and none is shown.
It has no published reliability or validity coefficients of its own; it adapts established, cited
paradigms and reports raw performance on a fixed form.
It measures, and it makes no claim to train, improve, protect against, or diagnose anything.
A result belongs to the person who produced it, and is never a criterion in a decision made about
them by anyone else.

The engine is pure and side-effect free, every score is hand-recomputable from the published
derivation, and every administered form is reconstructable from its seed.
